<LegalSection id="introduction" title="1. Introduction">
  At TASTO, we recognize that privacy is a core operational requirement. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, secure, and share personal information in connection with the TASTO platform, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge the terms of this Policy. We are committed to processing data responsibly and in alignment with global governance standards.

</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="information-we-collect" title="2. Information We Collect">
  We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to an individual. This includes the following categories of data:

- **Operational Account Information:** Names, business email addresses, billing coordinates, and credentials associated with organizational administrators and platform users.
- **System Telemetry & Usage Data:** Machine identifiers, IP addresses, browser configurations, access logs, and performance metrics gathered automatically during platform interactions.
- **Inquiry Communications:** Data supplied directly when requesting demonstrations, contacting support, or engaging in business development discussions.
</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="how-information-is-used" title="3. How Information Is Used">
  TASTO processes personal data solely to maintain operational stability and deliver enterprise-level infrastructure services. Specific purposes include:

- Provisioning, maintaining, and optimizing the TASTO platform.
- Verifying user identities and authenticating organizational access levels.
- Analyzing system health, diagnosing latency issues, and enforcing platform security protocols.
- Complying with statutory legal obligations and regulatory standards.
</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="platform-communications" title="4. Platform Communications">
  Operational communications are strictly limited to security advisories, system maintenance notifications, and critical administrative announcements.

We do not distribute marketing materials unless explicitly authorized by the subscribing organization. Users may manage communication preferences through their account settings.

</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="data-security" title="5. Data Security">
  We employ institutional-grade technical and organizational security measures to protect subscriber data.

All transport layers utilize TLS encryption, and data at rest is secured via AES-256 standards. Access control policies enforce the principle of least privilege, restricting telemetry review to authorized personnel only.

</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="third-party-services" title="6. Third-Party Services">
  TASTO does not sell, lease, or distribute data to third parties. We utilize vetted service providers (such as infrastructure hosts and database backups) to operate our platform.

These processors are contractually bound to process data only under our instructions and in compliance with security and privacy requirements.

</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="international-operations" title="7. International Operations">
  As a global enterprise platform, TASTO coordinates infrastructure across multiple jurisdictions. Data may be transferred to, and processed in, locations outside of your country of residence.

We ensure appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place to guarantee equivalence of protection.

</LegalSection>

<LegalSection id="policy-updates" title="8. Policy Updates">
  This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in regulatory standards or platform updates.

We will notify primary administrative contacts of significant alterations via email or platform dashboard banners prior to the changes taking effect.

</LegalSection>
